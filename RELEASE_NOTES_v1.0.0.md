## Translation and comparison

The MAO translation was written directly from the Japanese. George Henry
Shaft's English was introduced only after the MAO prose had been frozen for
comparison and audit; it was not used to create or revise the translation.

The completed comparison contains 38,637 comparable units, 6,899 confirmed
source-bound errors, 342 questionable cases, and 23,220 accepted translations.
Potential bindings were withheld wherever the evidence did not support a safe
alignment.

The separate annotated *On Cross Channel* edition preserves the complete work
in its original chapter and paragraph structure. Its 847 recorded findings are
attached to exact marked spans and can be read in context.
